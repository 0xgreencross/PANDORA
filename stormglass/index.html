THE RULES
One plate a day. Its price starts at the greater of the founding price and four times the median of the last seven sales, plus what the day’s witnesses paid, and halves every 2.4 hours; the day closes at 4:20pm in Miami. The first buy wins and chooses tomorrow’s plate from thirty-two. An unsold day is bought by the vault at the reference price if it can pay and held forever; if it cannot, the Tombstone is minted and the collection is dead.
Plate Zero is sold by candle: an ascending auction, every bid rerolling the plate, going out at a moment drawn from a block hash inside a six-hour window after the first day. The winning bid is the founding price, the price of a seat forever.
Witnesses pay any amount toward a day: a seat at the founding rate (one a day at most), a notch on the plate in the accent ink, and the day’s price rises by it. Seventy to the artist in the transaction, twenty to the seats, ten to the vault. Royalties 6.9% to the vault. No owner, no dials, nothing to expire: the engine, the print and the sky are on the chain. CC0. Greencross, always.
