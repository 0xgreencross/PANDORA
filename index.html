THE PROJECT

DITHERVOID // STORMGLASS is one plate a day at a falling price, the chain writing the sky, until it dies.

A plate is a kinetic monument: a stone, a solid, a growth or an assembly of them, turning once per loop under one sun, printed in ink and dither. The engine that draws it is not a file on a server; it is stored in the contract, byte for byte, and every plate is drawn again each time it is looked at. What changes between two lookings is the sky.

The sky is read from the chain itself. The base fee sets the weather, the clock sets the hour, the moon and the tide, the price of ether floods the ground, the stablecoins slipping off their peg counterfeit the impression, the blob fee brings the hunger. No oracle, no server, no owner, no dial to expire. One ERC-721 collection, CC0, Greencross, always.

THE PLATE
Monuments (obelisk, trilithon, ziggurat, dolmen, stelae, the gate, the sundial, the hourglass), the golden solids, the generators of the golden proportion (the tree, the nest, the whorl, the circle of stones, quartz), assemblies of them, and, one plate in a hundred, the STORMGLASS itself with the weather inside it.
THE PRINT
A loop of 36 frames at 1080 pixels, square by default; the glass also shows it tall (9:16) and wide (16:9). The print is written exactly as it is drawn: what you see on the glass is the file.

THE SALE

PLATE ZERO, BY CANDLE

An ascending auction. Every bid rerolls the plate; outbid, you are refunded at once. After the first day the flame goes out at a moment drawn from a block hash inside a six-hour window. The leader at the flame takes Plate Zero, and the winning bid becomes the founding price: the price of a seat, forever.

FOUNDERS’ DAY

The day after the candle. Pay anything: seats at the founding rate, uncapped, and a notch on Plate Zero. No token that day.

ONE PLATE A DAY

The price starts at the greater of the founding price and four times the median of the last seven sales, plus what the day’s witnesses paid, and halves every 2.4 hours. The first buy wins and chooses tomorrow’s plate from thirty-two. The day closes at 4:20pm in Miami.

WITNESSES AND SEATS

Pay any amount toward a day: a seat at the founding rate (one a day at most), a notch on the plate, and the day’s price rises by it. Seats are permanent, belong to the wallet, and share twenty percent of everything.

THE VAULT

An unsold day is bought by the vault at the reference price if it can pay, and held forever. Royalties, 6.9 percent, go to the vault.

THE TOMBSTONE

If the vault cannot pay, the Tombstone is minted and the collection is dead. Every plate before it lives on, reading the sky each time it is looked at.
