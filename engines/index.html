THE ENGINES

Every engine and workbench of DITHERVOID, archived under its version and left running as it ran that day. Two families: STORMGLASS, the plate engine at the root of this site, and PANDORA, the box it grew out of. Open one, tap, roll, print.

The archived pages are the exact bytes of their commit. What they need from elsewhere (a chain, a wallet, the gardens) may have moved on; the engine itself always runs.
